# Market context at project start (early October 2026)

Dated snapshot from Clarksons and Sparta material shared on 5 Oct 2026. Use for orientation
and sanity checks only; it will go stale. Do not hard-code these numbers into models.

## Crude tankers
- A Hormuz crisis since ~March 2026 cut MEG VLCC cargoes from ~130/month to under 50 (Mar–May),
  recovering to ~123 in September (Clarksons cargo counts). Red Sea (Yanbu) liftings appeared from March.
- Rates went to records anyway: fewer cargoes but much less effective supply (owners refusing the
  transit, war-risk cover, queuing outside the strait, longer Atlantic-to-Asia hauls replacing Gulf barrels).
- TD3C ~WS 1,240 on 5 Oct (TCE >$1m/day). Fujairah (outside Hormuz) fixtures ~WS 780–830: an observable
  inside/outside premium (compare in $/t or TCE; different flat rates).
- MEG list on 5 Oct: 212 ships to mid-Nov; of 38 with ETA ≤14 days, 7 open, 18 on subs, 10 held, 3 restricted.
- Floating storage ~68 VLCCs (well above 2022–25 norm). Dry dock elevated in September.
- Fleet: deliveries rising (Clarksons scenario ~62 VLCCs in 2027, ~4.8% fleet growth).
- Suezmax: WS 900–1,000 deals on subs; TD6 WS 920 (TCE ~$740k/d). Turkish Straits delays 1–3 days.
- TD22 (USG→China) $60.4m lumpsum ≈ $30/bbl vs WTI–Brent ~$11: US crude to China shut on spot freight.

## Clean tankers (Sparta, 23–28 Sep)
- Possible US diesel export ban: bearish TC14 immediately, bullish TD25 only with a lag.
- USGC MR list collapsed from 33 to 6 open ships in four days as cargoes were pulled forward.
- Sparta views: TC2 Q4 paper overvalued (arb into NY shut); TC14 2027 strip undervalued
  (forward diesel arbs open every month); TC7 supportive (Singapore diesel arbs into East/South Africa widening).

## Implications for modelling
- Treat 2026 as a distinct regime (war-risk/willingness premium). Include regime indicators or
  fit separately; validate on 2022–25 and 2026 separately.
- Distinguish what a rate is pricing: cargo flow, willing fleet, or risk premium. They revert on different clocks.
